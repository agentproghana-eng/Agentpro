Account & Data Control

Delete your AgentPro account

This public page lets you request permanent deletion of your AgentPro account even if you no longer have access to the app.

Request deletion without the app

Email AgentPro Support and clearly state that you want your AgentPro account permanently deleted.

Use the email address registered to your account where possible. Include the registered email address or phone number so that Support can locate the correct account and verify ownership.

Email deletion request

Support email: support@agentproghana.com

Protect your account: Do not send your password, Mobile Money PIN, transaction PIN, MFA recovery codes, or other secret authentication credentials by email.

If you can still use AgentPro

You can also initiate deletion directly in the app from Settings → Delete Account . The in-app flow requires your current password before deletion.

What happens after your request

  • AgentPro Support verifies that the request belongs to the account owner.
  • If the account has an open operational shift or unresolved financial activity, reconciliation may need to be completed before deletion can finish.
  • The account is permanently deleted and cannot be reactivated.
  • Active authentication sessions and account access are revoked.
  • Direct personal profile data that is not required to be retained is removed or anonymized.

Data removed

Account deletion removes or anonymizes direct personal account information that is no longer required, including profile and contact information and user-controlled content where applicable.

Data that may be retained

Limited records may be retained when necessary for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, accounting, regulatory, legal, or audit purposes.

These can include financial and transaction records, fraud-prevention records, security and audit records, and limited pseudonymous anti-abuse identifiers.

Retained records are not used to reactivate a permanently deleted AgentPro account.

Deleting your account does not reset previously used free-trial eligibility.

Need help?

Contact support@agentproghana.com and use the subject “AgentPro Account Deletion Request”.
